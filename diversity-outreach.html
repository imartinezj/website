Diversity & Outreach

Equity runs through my research and teaching — closing, rather than widening, existing social gaps as new transport technologies emerge. The activities below are where that commitment takes shape outside the lab and the classroom.

YoungITGirls

Member · 2020–2024

A Spain-based, volunteer-run association that encourages girls and young women into STEM, engineering, and technology careers, through mentoring, workshops, and outreach activities aimed at closing the gender gap in these fields — only around 29% of engineering students in Spain are women.

CEG Equity, Diversity & Inclusion and Academic Culture (EDI & AC) team

Member · since 2022

A faculty-wide group at TU Delft's Faculty of Civil Engineering and Geosciences, working across six themes: research & integrity, education & outreach, early-career researchers & supervision, reward systems, inclusive language & policies, and communication & events — shaping concrete actions toward an inclusive, equitable, and socially safe faculty environment.
