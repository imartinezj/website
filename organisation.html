Organisation

Workshops, tutorials, and committee meetings I have (co-)organised.

IEEE ITSC Workshop — Automated Mobility in Mixed Traffic (3rd edition)

Gold Coast, Australia · 18 November 2025

Co-organiser of the third edition of this workshop on data-driven, empirical, and AI-based research for mixed traffic of automated and human-driven vehicles, run alongside the new IEEE ITSS Technical Committee on Automated Mobility in Mixed Traffic.

itsc2025.mixedtraffic.org →

RERITE Tutorial — Reproducibility in Transportation Research: A Hands-on Tutorial

IEEE ITSC 2024 · Edmonton, Canada · 24 September 2024

Co-organiser, with Cathy Wu (MIT), Bidisha Ghosh (Trinity College Dublin), and Zuduo Zheng (University of Queensland), of a hands-on tutorial on reproducible research practices for the ITS community, under the RERITE working group.

rerite.org/itsc24-rr-tutorial →

IEEE ITSC Workshop — Automated Mobility in Emerging Mixed Traffic (2nd edition)

Edmonton, Canada · 24 September 2024

Co-organiser of the second edition of this workshop on datasets, modelling, and empirical research for automated vehicles coexisting with human-driven traffic.

itsc2024.mixedtraffic.org →

IEEE ITSC Workshop — Data-driven and Empirical Research for Emerging Mixed Traffic of AVs and HDVs

Bilbao, Spain · 24 September 2023

Co-organiser of the first edition of this workshop, bringing together researchers and data/vehicle-automation companies to discuss empirical and data-driven research on mixed automated/human traffic.

sites.google.com/view/itsc2023-mixed-traffic →

TFTC Summer Meeting — Traffic Flow Theory and Characteristics Committee (TRB ACP50)

AMS Institute, Amsterdam, The Netherlands · 26–28 July 2023

Co-organised this TRB standing-committee summer meeting, hosted by TU Delft with the Amsterdam Institute for Advanced Metropolitan Solutions, on the theme of urban traffic operations for resilient cities.
