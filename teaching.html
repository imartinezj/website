Teaching

Operations and Traffic Management

Teacher since 2026 · Unit 4 of Transport Systems Design (Course code: CIEM6250) · B-Module, Traffic and Transport Engineering track, MSc Civil Engineering

Focuses on the operational management of road traffic and other transport modes, including real-time public transport operations and crowd management. Covers control strategies that improve travel time and accessibility while balancing efficiency, safety, and sustainability, with growing attention to automation and vehicle connectivity.

Assessment: Project

Sustainability, Innovation and Transitions

Invited lecturer · MSc elective, Civil Engineering & TIL programmes · Course code: CIEM6303 · Since 2024, yearly

Transport and mobility are changing rapidly — partly through technological and societal developments, partly because society's demands on the transport system are shifting. This course explores that changing context along three lines: sustainability, innovation, and transitions, equipping students to develop and assess new ideas for transportation and mobility.

Designed and deliver a 2×45 min session on transportation justice and equity, covering Maslow’s hierarchy of needs and theories of justice grounded in concrete transport examples. Anchored by two case studies — the US Interstate Highway System, looking back at what went wrong, and vehicle automation, looking ahead through open discussion with students.

Intelligent Vehicles for Safe and Efficient Traffic: Design and Assessment

Coordinator since 2023 · Course code(s): CIEQ6223 / CIETIL6223

Intelligent Vehicle Systems are a key enabling technology for safer, more efficient, and more sustainable transport. Students take an intelligent vehicle system of their choice through the full workflow — from system definition and controller design to performance evaluation and impact assessment — combining group project work with individual technical assessment, using Python throughout.

Assessment: (Group) Project + Exam

Mobility & Transport Seminars

Invited lecturer, hEAT lab · Bachelor minor in Transport · Course code: CT3520 · Since 2022, yearly

A day coordinated on behalf of the hEAT lab, introducing students to automated driving technology, including a hands-on session where they meet the department's research vehicles and talk with the technicians behind them. Colleagues from the hEAT lab — including PhD candidates and postdocs — join to present their own research in a rotating “parallel classroom” format.

Teach 3h yearly; coordinate hEAT lab colleagues for the parallel-classroom sessions.
